THE THREE PILLARS

AI & REASONING

AI & REASONING Exploring how intelligent systems reason, debate, evaluate, and refine ideas. My research focuses on multi-agent systems, argumentative quality evaluation, and the emerging intersection between artificial intelligence and human-style judgement. I am particularly interested in how reasoning quality can be measured, improved, and operationalised within real-world decision systems.

SYSTEMS & AUTOMATION

SYSTEMS & AUTOMATION Designing scalable software architectures and automation systems that reduce operational friction and enable organisations to move faster with greater reliability. My work spans enterprise software engineering, process automation, intelligent workflows, and the practical integration of AI into business environments where resilience, maintainability, and long-term scalability matter.

VALUE & STRATEGY

VALUE & STRATEGY Combining a background in finance, valuation, and software engineering to understand how systems influence enterprise value over time. My focus is on building technologies and operational structures that improve decision-making, reduce risk, strengthen scalability, and create compounding strategic advantages for businesses operating in increasingly complex environments.
